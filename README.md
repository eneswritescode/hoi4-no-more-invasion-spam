This mod fixes Hearts of Iron IV's flawed AI naval invasion logic. It completely eliminates constant invasion micro-spam and forces the AI to plan, organize, and execute serious, concentrated amphibious assaults with strategic cooldowns.

Key Features

* Minor Country Invasion Restrictions: Minor nations (non-major countries) are restricted from spamming naval invasions. They will no longer send suicidal 1-division landings across oceans.
* 6-Month Cooldown on Failed Targets (180 Days): If the AI fails an invasion attempt at a coastline or port, it is forced to wait 180 days (up from vanilla's 60 days) before evaluating that area again.
* Strict Preparation & Readiness Requirements: The AI will no longer launch half-baked invasions. It now requires at least 75% plan value (up from 30%), 85% unit organization (up from 40%), and 80% unit readiness (up from 25%) before order execution.
* No More 1-Division Micro-Spam: Increases coastal province limits per order from 24 to 60, preventing the AI from creating dozens of single-province suicide landings.
* Concentrated Strike Forces: Multiplies AI division weighting for invasion orders by 1.5x, forcing the AI to assemble real task forces rather than feeding single divisions to coastal forts.
* Realistic Naval Superiority Evaluation: Restricts the AI from attempting invasions if enemy fleet superiority is over 1.5x.

Compatibility

* Game Version: Compatible with HOI4 v1.19.* (and all future sub-versions).
* Ironman / Achievement Compatible: Changes AI defines and strategy scripts, modifying game checksum.
* Mod Compatibility: Fully compatible with overhaul mods (Road to 56, Kaiserreich, Millennium Dawn, Expert AI, etc.).

How to Use

Simply subscribe to the mod, enable No More Invasion Spam: Smart AI in your Paradox Launcher playset, and start your game!
